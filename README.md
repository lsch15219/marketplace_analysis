📊 Marketplace Metrics Analysis

Analytical study of a marketplace’s key metrics for 2024. 
Focus on user behavior, revenue, acquisition channels, unit economics, and AB testing results.

🎯 Objective

Identify potential issues, warning signals, and growth opportunities

Analyze monetization and unit economics

Evaluate marketing effectiveness and AB test results

📂 Data

Users: registration, demographics, region, acquisition channel, buyer segment

Events: user interactions (page views, clicks, add-to-cart, searches, checkouts)

Orders: products, quantity, price, category

Campaign_costs: marketing budget by channel and month

Period: Jan 1 - Dec 31, 2024

🛠️ Analysis Steps

Metrics overview (DAU/MAU, Stickiness, Retention, Conversion)

Monetization & unit economics (Revenue, AOV, ARPU, ARPPU, CAC, ROI, Payback Period)

Insights and growth opportunities (funnel, channel effectiveness, cohort analysis)

AB test analysis (TikTok first-purchase bonus)

📈 Key Insights

📉 Decline in Stickiness and purchase conversion from April 2024

Revenue drop due to fewer orders, ARPPU stable, ARPU decreasing

Weakest channels: TikTok, Email; strongest: Affiliate, Google Ads

AB test: first-purchase bonus increased conversion 1% → 7.5% and 10x ARPU

🚀 Tools & Technologies

Python: pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, statsmodels
